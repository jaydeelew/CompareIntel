## Linear Algebra with Lists
Properties of the matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$:
- Determinant: $\det(A) = ad - bc$
- Trace: $\text{tr}(A) = a + d$
- Inverse (if exists):
  $$A^{-1} = \frac{1}{ad - bc}\begin{pmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{pmatrix}$$
