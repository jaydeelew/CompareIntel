# Test Prompt: Casual Plain-Text Math

A student texted you: why is tan(60°) equal to the square root of 3, and why is sin(60°) that same square root over 2? They also asked about a unit cube (the face diagonal and the space diagonal) and about an equilateral triangle with side length 2, whose height they think is the square root of 3.

Reply like a helpful tutor in an informal chat. Use ordinary sentences only. Do not use markdown, LaTeX dollar signs, backslash-paren delimiters, or any other special formatting — type the math the way you would in a plain text message. Please include the height, the two diagonals, tan(60°), and sin(60°) in those sentences.

Along the way, mention that a poster of the 30-60-90 triangle costs $3 plus $1.50 shipping, and that the same square-root value is what Python prints for math.sqrt(3).
