### **Math Basics Demonstration**

1. **Inline Math (Quadratic Formula):**
   The quadratic formula solves for the roots of a quadratic equation \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\). It is given by:
   \(x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}\), where \(a \neq 0\).

2. **Display Math (Aligned Equations):**
   Solve the system of equations:
   $$\begin{align}
   x + y &= 5 \\
   2x - y &= 1
   \end{align}$$

3. **Matrices and Cases:**
   The identity matrix and a piecewise function:
   $$I = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, \quad
   f(x) = \begin{cases}
   x^2 & \text{if } x \geq 0 \\
   -x & \text{if } x < 0
   \end{cases}$$

4. **Multiple Equations:**
   Fundamental physics equations:
   $$E = mc^2$$
   $$F = ma$$
   $$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$$
