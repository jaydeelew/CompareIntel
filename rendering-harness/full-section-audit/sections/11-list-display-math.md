## Scenario 1: Math in Lists with Formatting
1. The **Pythagorean theorem** states that for any right triangle, the relationship between the lengths of the sides is given by the formula:
   \[
   a^2 + b^2 = c^2
   \]
2. Let’s consider a *right triangle* with side lengths \(a = 3\) and \(b = 4\):
   - To find the hypotenuse \(c\):
   \[
   c = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{9 + 16} = \sqrt{25} = 5
   \]
   - Therefore, the length of the hypotenuse \(c\) is \(5\) units.
