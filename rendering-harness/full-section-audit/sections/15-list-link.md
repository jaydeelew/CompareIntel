1. **Inline Math: Quadratic Formula**

   The quadratic formula is given by:

   \[
   x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
   \]

   Here, \( b^2 - 4ac \) is called the discriminant. If the discriminant is positive, there are two distinct real roots. If it is zero, there is exactly one real root. If it is negative, the roots are complex numbers.

2. **Display Math with Aligned Equations**

   $$
   \begin{align}
   x + y &= 5 \\
   2x - y &= 1
   \end{align}
   $$

   To solve this system, you can use substitution or elimination methods.

   f(x) = \begin{cases}
   x^2 & \text{if } x \geq 0 \\
   -x & \text{if } x < 0
   \end{cases}
   $$

   $$
   E = mc^2 \\
   F = ma \\
   a^2 + b^2 = c^2

   - \( F = ma \) is Newton's second law of motion.
   - \( a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \) is the Pythagorean theorem.
