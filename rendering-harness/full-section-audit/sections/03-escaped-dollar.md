## Real World Context
> **Note**: In economics, the cost function \( C(q) = 100 + 5q \) indicates that the fixed costs are \$100 and the variable costs are \$5 per unit produced.

### Money Context
If you buy 10 units:
- Fixed Cost: \$100
- Variable Cost for 10 units: \( 5 \times 10 = 50 \)

Thus, the total cost is:
$$ C(10) = 100 + 50 = 150 $$

In summary, the total cost of purchasing 10 units is **\$150**.
