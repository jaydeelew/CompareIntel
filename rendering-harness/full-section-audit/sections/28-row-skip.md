Here’s a compact “cheat-sheet” that illustrates several common LaTeX patterns in a single response.

1. Inline math  
   The quadratic formula $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$ gives the two (real or complex) roots of a quadratic equation $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$.

2. Display math with aligned equations  
   $$
   \begin{align}
   x + y &= 5 \\
   2x - y &= 1
   \end{align}
   $$

3. Matrices and cases  
   $$
   I = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad
   f(x) = \begin{cases}
     x^2 & \text{if } x \ge 0,\\[6pt]
     -x  & \text{if } x < 0.
   \end{cases}
   $$

4. Multiple standalone equations  
   $$
   E = mc^2
   $$
   $$
   F = ma
   $$
   $$
   a^2 + b^2 = c^2
   $$

5. Link for further reading  
   See the Wikipedia article on the quadratic formula: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_formula

6. Money context (not rendered as math)  
   The item costs $5 and the shipping is $10, for a total of $15.  
   Budget: \$1000.
