## Further Calculus: Optimization
To find the local extrema of a function $g(x) = -\frac{1}{3}x^3 + x^2$, we set the derivative to zero:

1. **Calculate the derivative**:
   $$g'(x) = -x^2 + 2x$$
2. **Find critical points**:
   Setting $g'(x) = 0 \implies -x(x - 2) = 0$.
   Thus, the critical points are at **$x = 0$** and **$x = 2$**.
